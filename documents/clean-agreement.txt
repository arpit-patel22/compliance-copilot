DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT

1. Purpose. The Processor shall use personal data solely to provide the email delivery services described in Annex I, and for no other purpose.

2. Third Parties. Personal data may be shared only with sub-processors listed in Annex II, each bound by a written data processing agreement imposing equivalent obligations.

3. Data Subject Rights. Data subjects may request deletion of their personal data at any time by contacting privacy@company.example; requests will be honored within 30 days.

4. Transfers. Data transfers outside the EEA shall occur only under Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission.

5. Retention. Personal data shall be retained no longer than 24 months after the end of the service term, then securely deleted.
