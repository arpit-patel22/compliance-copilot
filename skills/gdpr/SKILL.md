# GDPR Analysis Procedure

STEP 1 - Call search_clauses with these keywords:
personal data, consent, erasure, deletion, third parties, processing, transfer, retention

STEP 2 - Evaluate each matching clause against these requirements:
- Lawful basis for processing clearly stated? (Art. 6) - missing or "any purpose" = HIGH
- Users CAN request deletion of their data? (Art. 17) - any clause preventing deletion = HIGH
- Purpose of processing limited and specific? (Art. 5) - "any commercial purpose" = HIGH
- Third-party sharing has safeguards or a processor agreement? (Art. 28) - sharing without safeguards = HIGH
- Data retention limits defined? (Art. 5) - indefinite retention = MEDIUM
- International transfers addressed with protections? (Chapter V) - unrestricted global transfer = MEDIUM

STEP 3 - For each finding, cite the article EXACTLY as written above (e.g., "GDPR Art. 17"), assign risk HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW, and write a one-line fix.
