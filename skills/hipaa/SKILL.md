# HIPAA Analysis Procedure

STEP 1 - Call search_clauses with these keywords:
health information, medical, protected, disclosure, PHI, records, safeguards, breach

STEP 2 - Evaluate each matching clause against these requirements:
- Administrative/physical/technical safeguards for PHI described? (Security Rule) - absent = HIGH
- Business associate agreements required for vendors handling PHI? - absent = HIGH
- Breach notification procedure defined? - absent = MEDIUM
- Access controls and minimum-necessary standard stated? (Privacy Rule) - absent = MEDIUM
- Patient rights to access their records acknowledged? - denied = HIGH

STEP 3 - For each finding, cite the rule EXACTLY as written above (e.g., "HIPAA Security Rule"), assign risk HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW, and write a one-line fix.
